Privacy Policy

Last update: January 2026

This information notice describes how Studio Eclettica manages its website with regard to the processing of personal data of users who consult it, submit applications, or subscribe to the newsletter.

1. Data Controller

The Data Controller is Studio Eclettica, with registered office at Corso Sempione 27/E, 20015 Parabiago (MI), VAT No. 03600040962.
For any information, you may write to: info@studioeclettica.it.

2. Types of Data Processed and Purposes

A. Data provided by the user (Contact Form)
The optional, explicit, and voluntary sending of messages to the contact addresses or via forms entails the acquisition of the sender's address, which is necessary to reply to requests, as well as any other personal data included in the message.

B. Spontaneous Applications and "Work with Us"
By sending their Curriculum Vitae via the appropriate sections or by email, the user authorizes the processing of personal data (personal details, contact information, educational and professional background) exclusively for personnel selection purposes. CVs will be retained for a maximum period of 24 months.

C. Newsletter and Marketing
Subject to explicit consent (via the appropriate checkbox), data (email address) will be used to send informative newsletters and updates on collections. It is possible to unsubscribe at any time by clicking the link present in every email.

D. Navigation Data
The computer systems and software procedures used to operate this website acquire, during their normal operation, some personal data whose transmission is implicit in the use of Internet communication protocols (e.g., IP addresses, access times). These data are used solely to obtain anonymous statistical information.

3. Legal Basis

Data processing occurs based on the user's consent (for newsletters and CV submission) and for the execution of pre-contractual measures or legitimate interest (to respond to contact requests).

4. Processing Methods and Duration

Personal data are processed using automated tools for the time strictly necessary to achieve the purposes for which they were collected. Specific security measures are observed to prevent data loss, illicit or incorrect use, and unauthorized access.

5. Rights of Data Subjects

Users have the right at any time to:

  • Ask for confirmation of the existence or non-existence of their personal data;
  • Obtain indications regarding the purposes of the processing, the categories of personal data, the recipients or categories of recipients to whom the personal data have been or will be disclosed, and, where possible, the retention period;
  • Obtain the rectification and erasure of data;
  • Obtain the restriction of processing;
  • Revoke consent to processing (e.g., unsubscribe from the newsletter).
