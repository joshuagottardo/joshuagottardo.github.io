Beyond
The Sketch.

We are not just designers. We are engineers of creativity. Since 1990, Studio Eclettica has defined the standards in footwear and leather goods design, combining artistic intuition with industrial precision.

The Origin

"Eclectic" is not just a name, it is our method. It means not following a single path, but harmonizing the best principles from different systems.

We were born with the idea that style without technique is just a drawing, and technique without style is just a product. We position ourselves exactly in the middle: where the magic happens.

The Vision

We operate in the three most important footwear districts in Italy: Milan, Padua, and the Marche region. This widespread presence allows us to draw upon diverse traditions, unique materials, and specific artisanal skills for every type of product.

The Future

Today, we look ahead by integrating 3D prototyping and sustainability into our processes, guaranteeing luxury brands a partner who speaks the language of innovation.
